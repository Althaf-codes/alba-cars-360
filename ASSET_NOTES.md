# Public concept assets
## Interactive Porsche 360° demonstration media

- Vehicle reference: [ALBA CARS 2025 Porsche 911 Carrera listing](https://albacars.ae/buy-used-cars/vehicle/1808-porsche-carrera--911-carrera).
- Third-party source model: **Porsche 911 992** by Kentik khudosovtsev, via [Blendkit](https://www.blendkit.com/asset-gallery-detail/c2015e97-3521-4966-bfb5-e6076ba5cdd5/).
- Licence: Blendkit Royalty Free. The detailed source and derivative-render record is retained in `tools/porsche360-render/asset-source.md`.
- The source `.blend` model is deliberately excluded from this repository. The committed media consists only of independently rendered derivative demonstration images.
- Output: 24 azimuth positions × 3 elevations = 72 WebP frames in `public/360/porsche-911-carrera-14036ac/` (approximately 2.97 MiB total).
- Blender was used only as an offline asset-generation tool. The website ships neither Blender nor a runtime WebGL, Three.js, or other 3D viewer.
- The interactive turntable is independently rendered demonstration media and is not official ALBA CARS vehicle photography. Porsche and ALBA CARS marks remain the property of their respective owners; this concept is not affiliated with either party.
